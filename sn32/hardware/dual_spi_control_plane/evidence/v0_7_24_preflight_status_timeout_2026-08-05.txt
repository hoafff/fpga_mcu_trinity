SN32 -> P1/P2 dual-SPI qualification evidence
Firmware: v0.7.24 / build 0x00070018
Date recorded: 2026-08-05
Result: FAIL

Observed command (three attempts):

  trinity-host --port COM3 sn32-qualify --timeout 10 --poll 0.1 --liveness 10

Observed output:

  [PREFLIGHT_PING]
  result=PASS
  uptime_ms=7225
  FAIL: timed out waiting for SN32 response

  [PREFLIGHT_PING]
  result=PASS
  uptime_ms=10773
  FAIL: timed out waiting for SN32 response

  [PREFLIGHT_PING]
  result=PASS
  uptime_ms=4733
  FAIL: timed out waiting for SN32 response

Boundary established by the host command order:

  1. PING completed and its response decoded.
  2. The next request was GET_SYSTEM_STATUS.
  3. No GET_SYSTEM_STATUS response frame arrived before the host deadline.
  4. SPI_FIRST_FAILURE was never requested and no live SPI diagnostic or KAT
     operation began.

Source audit finding:

  - GET_SYSTEM_STATUS itself remained a side-effect-free local snapshot.
  - main() checked the periodic-probe deadline immediately after pc_poll(), so
    a due background probe could begin in the same iteration that returned the
    PING response.
  - The v0.7.24 GPIO-SPI inner bit loop did not call progress(). PC UART uses
    polled ingress, so the immediately following request could remain unread
    throughout the background transfer and be lost to RX overrun.

Corrective boundary for v0.7.25:

  - pump UART ingress after every SPI bit only while SCK is low;
  - keep progress() queue-only, with no nested command execution;
  - require no partial/queued PC frame and 250 ms of PC inactivity before an
    automatic periodic probe may start.

This evidence does not qualify P1, P2, dual-SPI, session, telemetry, Tiny,
ML-KEM or the full system. The source-audit diagnosis remains to be confirmed
by the v0.7.25 one-shot cold-boot hardware qualification.
